Six templates, one source of truth

Your content is stored once and typeset by whichever design you pick, so switching never loses a word. These previews are rendered live from the same code the editor uses.

Built for the boring parts of job hunting

True-to-print live preview

The preview is the page: real A4 or Letter dimensions, with a marker where each printed page ends.

PDFs that parsers can read

“Save as PDF” uses your browser’s print engine for crisp vector text — not a screenshot — so applicant-tracking systems can extract every line.

Shrink to one page

Just over a page? One switch scales type and spacing down by exactly as much as needed, and no further.

Coaching, not just fields

A resume-strength meter suggests the next most useful improvement; autocomplete knows hundreds of titles, degrees and skills.

Autosave and version history

Every change is saved as you type. Roll back to any of the last fifteen versions of each resume.

Keyboard & screen-reader friendly

Semantic forms, labelled controls, focus management and shortcuts — reorder entries without ever touching a mouse.

Your resume never leaves your device

Folio has no server, no accounts and no analytics. Resumes are saved in your browser’s local storage; fonts are bundled, so the app makes no third-party requests at all. Export a JSON backup any time to move between browsers.
